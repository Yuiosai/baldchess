Four-player chess: rules and how to play online

Four-player chess brings four sides to a cross-shaped board. On BaldChess, you can create a private room, invite friends by a six-character code, and play either free-for-all or in teams.

Start a game with friends

No registration is needed. Choose 4 PLAYERS, set your preferred time limit and board setup, create a room, and share the invite link or code.

Play four-player chess

How is the board different?

Instead of the standard 8×8 board, BaldChess uses a 14×14 cross-shaped board: the corners are removed, leaving space for pieces belonging to four players. Each player begins on a different side. The colors are red, blue, yellow and green.

What is the move order?

Turns follow a repeating order: red → blue → yellow → green. Players choose a piece and move it to a legal square. Available moves appear when a piece is selected; you can click squares or drag pieces.

Free-for-all or teams

  • Free-for-all: each of the four colors plays for itself.
  • Teams: the host can arrange sides as two versus two, three versus one, or other supported groups. Teammates cannot capture each other's pieces.

Time controls and board options

Choose from untimed games or time controls including 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 30 and 60 minutes. You can add a per-move increment or give different time limits to individual seats. Select the standard starting position, Fischer random arrangement, or build a custom board. Players can choose visual board themes.

Can you play one-on-one?

Yes. BaldChess also has a classic 1 vs 1 mode on an 8×8 board. You can switch modes when configuring a new match in the same lobby, subject to the available seats.

Does BaldChess use standard four-player chess rules?

BaldChess is its own casual implementation. Its check, elimination, and draw handling may differ from other four-player chess platforms. Pieces belonging to eliminated players remain as inactive obstacles. It is designed for friendly private games rather than official tournament adjudication.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to register?

No. Create a room and share its link with the other players.

Does it work on a phone?

The game interface adapts to smartphones and desktops. For the best experience, keep a stable connection throughout the match.

Can I play another match in the same room?

Yes. The host can start another match and change game settings without sending everyone a new room code.
